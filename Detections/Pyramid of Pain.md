The [Pyramid of Pain](https://detect-respond.blogspot.com/2013/03/the-pyramid-of-pain.html), is a framework that categorizes detection indicators by the damage they cause to an attacker when you successfully detect and respond to them. The higher you operate, the more time, effort, and resources you force them to burn.

Pyramid of Pain
```
TTPs
	Tools
        Net/Host Artifacts
			Domain Names
	            IP Addresses
		            Hash Values    
```
